Academic Excellence & Financial Grants

Empowering Future Leaders.

St. Cecilia's College is committed to supporting deserving students through financial assistance programs, merit-based grants, and community leadership awards designed to help students achieve their academic goals.

  • Employee's Dependents Scholarship (Admin Scholar): 100% discount on tuition, miscellaneous, laboratory, and enrollment fees.
  • Tuition Fee Discount for Other Relatives: 50% tuition fee discount for qualified relatives, including nephews, nieces, and first cousins.
  • Sports and Athletics Scholarship: 25% to 100% free tuition based on tournament level (Municipal, Provincial, Regional, or National), plus free jerseys for basketball and volleyball.
  • UniFAST Scholarship: Financial assistance of PHP 13,500 per semester for four years, under the applicable CHED UniFAST program and Republic Act No. 10931.
  • Working Scholar Program: Operational and administrative work-study opportunities across school offices.
  • Academic Scholarship Program: Tuition discounts from 50% to 100%, based on GWA: 1.00–1.20 for 100%, 1.30–1.50 for 75%, and 1.60–1.80 for 50%.
