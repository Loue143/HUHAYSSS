1. Employee's Dependents Scholarship (Admin Scholar)

100% discount on tuition, miscellaneous, laboratory, and enrollment fees.

View Requirements

Qualifications

  • Must be an eligible dependent under the institution's scholarship policy.
  • Eligible relationships and the maximum number of dependents are subject to employee policy.
  • The required employee service period must be verified with the administration.
  • Subject to applicable institutional policies and official memoranda.

2. Tuition Fee Discount for Other Relatives

50% tuition fee discount for qualified relatives, including nephews, nieces, and first cousins.

View Requirements

Qualifications

  • Must meet the school's approved relationship requirements.
  • The employee's length of regular service must be verified.
  • Eligibility and supporting documents are subject to administrative review.
🏆

3. Sports and Athletics Scholarship Program

25% to 100% free tuition based on tournament level for qualified varsity athletes.

View Benefits & Requirements

Benefits by tournament level

  • National level — 100% free tuition.
  • Regional level — 75% free tuition.
  • Provincial level — 50% free tuition.
  • Municipal or selected players — 25% free tuition.

General requirements

  • Must be a bona fide student of the college.
  • Must attend scheduled practices and team activities.
  • Must remain an active varsity member.
  • Must meet the required academic standing and conduct rules.
🎓

4. UniFAST Scholarship

Financial assistance of PHP 13,500 per semester for eligible students under the applicable program.

View Requirements

Potential supporting documents

  • Required ID photographs.
  • Birth certificate and other identity documents, as required.

  • Additional income or program-specific documents may be required.

Confirm current eligibility rules and the document checklist with the scholarship office.

5. Working Scholar Program

Work-study opportunities supporting administrative and operational school offices.

View Requirements

General requirements

  • Must follow institutional rules and regulations.
  • Must meet the required general weighted average.
  • Must satisfy grade and attendance requirements.
  • Must demonstrate good moral character and commitment to assigned duties.

Documents that may be requested

  • Scholarship application form and school ID.
  • Recent ID photograph.
  • Previous grades or report card.
  • Study load and additional documents requested by the office.
📚

6. Academic Scholarship Program

Tuition discounts from 50% to 100%, based on general weighted average (GWA).

View Benefits & Requirements

GWA-based benefits

  • GWA 1.00–1.20 — 100% tuition discount.
  • GWA 1.30–1.50 — 75% tuition discount.
  • GWA 1.60–1.80 — 50% tuition discount.

General requirements

  • Must meet the GWA requirement for the selected category.
  • Must satisfy the school's grade requirements.
  • Must maintain the required academic load and enrollment status.
  • Renewal depends on the institution's current scholarship policies.
